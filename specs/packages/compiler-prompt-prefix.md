# compiler-prompt-prefix: Prefix-First Prompt Layout

## Intent

This package governs the prompt-prefix pass of [DR-065](../decisions/065-prompt-prefix-pass.md): the compile-time reorder that puts an acting prompt's static instructions ahead of its relayed runtime values, its deterministic tool, and the fidelity acceptance of the reordered items.

## External Behavior

### compiler-prompt-prefix-1

When the prefix pass rewrites an eligible acting item, the pass shall move every standalone relay block — a maximal run of prompt lines beginning with the literal quote marker [[playbook-5](playbook.md#playbook-5)] bounded by blank prompt lines or the blockquote's edges — after the item's last instruction line, keeping every non-blank prompt line byte-for-byte, the instruction lines in their order with one blank line separating them from the relays, and the relay blocks in their order with one blank line between consecutive blocks, and shall leave the item's heading, condition, acting clause, and `Results:` metadata, every other item, and every non-item section other than a legacy `## Prefixed prompts` section [[compiler-prompt-prefix-3](#compiler-prompt-prefix-3)] byte-for-byte.
The pass shall replace each maximal run of moved blocks and their bounding blank lines with that run's first blank line where it separated two remaining prompt lines and with nothing where it led or trailed them, drop any other blank line leading or trailing the remaining lines, and keep every other blank line byte-for-byte in place.

### compiler-prompt-prefix-2

Where an item is a script item, has no standalone relay block preceding an instruction line, or carries an instruction whose meaning depends on a relayed value standing before it, the pass shall leave the item unchanged, and the pass shall add no prompt line, label, or separator to any item.

### compiler-prompt-prefix-3

The pass shall add nothing beside the rewritten prompts and shall remove every `## Prefixed prompts` section of the source through the line before the next heading, together with the blank lines before it where no heading follows; when it rewrites no item and the source carries no such section, the target shall equal the source.

### compiler-prompt-prefix-4

When invoked with `--source <gears.md>` and `--target <gears.md>` and zero or more `--keep <ITEM-ID>`, the `slc/prefix-prompts.mjs` tool shall write to the target the source rewritten by the mechanical rules [[compiler-prompt-prefix-1](#compiler-prompt-prefix-1)], [[compiler-prompt-prefix-3](#compiler-prompt-prefix-3)] for every eligible item not named by `--keep`, preserving the source's line-ending convention and leaving the source unmodified, and shall print the rewritten item IDs or report that no item was eligible.

### compiler-prompt-prefix-5

When the Source-to-GEARS fidelity checker evaluates an item, the checker shall accept it in the Source-order layout, every complete occurrence of an authored fragment its prompt carries admitting an attribution in Source order [[playbook-1](playbook.md#playbook-1)] — an occurrence inside a longer carried one belonging to that one, identical fragments being alternative attributions of one occurrence, and only disjoint occurrences ordering each other — or, unless it is a script item, in the prefix-first layout, reading each authored fragment as units — a quoted relay fragment as one relay unit, and in an instruction or prompt fragment each paragraph of only quoted lines a relay unit and the lines between relay units, without their bounding blank lines, one instruction unit keeping its interior blank lines — and requiring that no standalone relay block precede an instruction line and that whole fragments taken in Source order tile its prompt — their instruction units before the trailing relay blocks — before each unit exactly the one blank line the pass leaves where a moved relay unit stood, exactly the authored blank lines after a unit of its fragment that kept its place, or the boundary Source composed before a fragment — and their relay units among the trailing blocks or, where the raw layout kept a relay beside instruction text, in place — each unit occurrence used exactly once, blank lines and bare relay lines free among the trailing blocks, and every alternative tiling kept as the fragment texts it carries; shall report an item accepted in neither layout and an item outside the Source-order layout whose alternative tilings exceed what the checker enumerates; and shall count for each item the fragments of one layout it is accepted in — those it carries contiguously, or those of one tiling — and for an item accepted in neither its contiguous ones, choosing the layouts so that the items together supply each authored fragment text as many times as Source authors it, one occurrence never standing for two authored fragments, and reading each item's layout from its prompt alone, a `## Prefixed prompts` section carrying no meaning:

- The units are the blocks the pass moves or keeps [[compiler-prompt-prefix-1](#compiler-prompt-prefix-1)], so a quoted block set apart by blank lines inside a fenced instruction is accepted at the tail like a separate relay, while a quoted line beside instruction text stays with it.

## Verification

### compiler-prompt-prefix-6

When the integration suite runs the shipped tool over each maintained workflow's GEARS and over a pre-image with each rewritten item's trailing relays moved back before its instructions, it shall verify that the tool leaves the GEARS, which carries no `## Prefixed prompts` section, unchanged, that the pre-image's rewritten set is exactly the items whose relays precede an instruction and its target is the GEARS byte for byte, every other item and the `## Optimizations` section included, that the GEARS with a legacy `## Prefixed prompts` section appended, or inserted before its `## Optimizations` section, yields the GEARS, and that a kept item is skipped [[compiler-prompt-prefix-1](#compiler-prompt-prefix-1)], [[compiler-prompt-prefix-2](#compiler-prompt-prefix-2)], [[compiler-prompt-prefix-3](#compiler-prompt-prefix-3)], [[compiler-prompt-prefix-4](#compiler-prompt-prefix-4)]; and that the shipped definition's example rewrites through the tool exactly as printed [[compiler-prompt-prefix-1](#compiler-prompt-prefix-1)].
When the integration suite runs the shipped tool over the output of a run with `--keep`, it shall verify that the second run rewrites only the kept item and yields the text of one run without `--keep` [[compiler-prompt-prefix-4](#compiler-prompt-prefix-4)].

### compiler-prompt-prefix-7

When the integration suite composes a rewritten item's prompt through the real runtime composer with two different relayed values, it shall verify that the two prompts share the complete instruction text as a common prefix while the original layout shares only the relay label [[compiler-prompt-prefix-1](#compiler-prompt-prefix-1)].

### compiler-prompt-prefix-8

When the integration suite runs the fidelity checker over each maintained source and its tool-rewritten GEARS, it shall verify zero findings, also with two legacy `## Prefixed prompts` sections appended that list its rewritten items, each of its nested-call items, an unknown ID, and a malformed entry, and over mutants — a rewritten item with a relay dropped, the same item's relays moved back before its first instruction, and those relays moved between two of its instruction paragraphs — it shall verify a dropped-fragment finding, zero findings for that Source-order layout, and a dropped-fragment finding, respectively [[compiler-prompt-prefix-5](#compiler-prompt-prefix-5)]; over a Source that authors a relay after its instruction, it shall verify that the relay moved before the instruction is reported out of Source order [[compiler-prompt-prefix-5](#compiler-prompt-prefix-5)]; over a Source that authors a relay, an instruction, a second relay, and a second instruction, it shall verify that the tool moves both relays after the instructions [[compiler-prompt-prefix-1](#compiler-prompt-prefix-1)] and the result has zero findings, while the second relay kept before the second instruction with only the first trailing is reported out of Source order [[compiler-prompt-prefix-5](#compiler-prompt-prefix-5)]; over the maintained GEARS with DECIDE-3's trailing relay block repeated after itself and CODE-3's first relay block repeated after its last, it shall verify that each item is reported out of Source order [[compiler-prompt-prefix-5](#compiler-prompt-prefix-5)]; over a Source that authors a relay before its instruction, it shall verify that a script item in that order has zero findings and with the instruction first is reported out of Source order, while a player or Captain item with the instruction first has zero findings [[compiler-prompt-prefix-5](#compiler-prompt-prefix-5)]; and over a Source whose fenced instruction opens with a blank-separated quoted block, it shall verify that the tool moves the block, the checker accepts the result with nothing listed, and a quoted line beside instruction text stays in place [[compiler-prompt-prefix-1](#compiler-prompt-prefix-1)], [[compiler-prompt-prefix-5](#compiler-prompt-prefix-5)].

### compiler-prompt-prefix-9

When the integration suite runs the shipped tool and the fidelity checker over Sources in which two items share an instruction paragraph, one instruction repeats a paragraph, one instruction repeats a relay block, one instruction carries an authored run of two blank lines, a relay-last item shares a relay with a relay-first one, one item's two instruction fragments are joined by two blank lines, two items' fragments mirror each other, bare relays authored through prose precede an instruction alone and beside another, Source joins two instructions, two relays, and a relay with an instruction without a blank line, a relay kept in place precedes and follows two authored blank lines, seven items are authored with one prompt, and sixty-five are with all but one kept, it shall verify that the authored run survives the rewrite while the blank lines bounding a moved block collapse to one [[compiler-prompt-prefix-1](#compiler-prompt-prefix-1)], that each rewritten result has zero findings, and that the checker reports a rewritten result with one repeated relay block deleted, with the authored run shortened, removed, or lengthened, with the relay-first item deleted beside the relay-last one sharing its relay, with a mirrored item's counterpart deleted, with one fragment of a two-fragment prompt deleted, with one of the seven items deleted, and with the authored blank lines beside a kept relay shortened or lengthened [[compiler-prompt-prefix-5](#compiler-prompt-prefix-5)].
